Head Voice Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Head Voice plays your own voice into your headphones as you sing or speak, so you can hear yourself over the music. Nothing about you or your voice is ever sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What Head Voice collects

Nothing. Head Voice has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising, and it makes no network connection. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information, usage data, or identifiers of any kind.

Your voice

Head Voice uses the microphone only to play your voice into your headphones in real time. The audio is processed on your device as it happens. It is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Third parties

Head Voice contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send the developer anonymized crash reports under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Head Voice does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

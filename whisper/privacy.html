Whisper Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Whisper lets two people sitting side by side talk quietly through their headphones in a loud place. The two iPhones link directly to each other over Bluetooth. Nothing about you or your conversation is ever sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What Whisper collects

Nothing. Whisper has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising, and it makes no internet connection at all. It works in airplane mode with Bluetooth on. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information, usage data, or identifiers of any kind.

Your voice

While a conversation is open, Whisper uses the microphone to send your voice to the one other phone you connected to, and plays their voice into your headphones. Audio goes straight from phone to phone over Bluetooth and is played as it arrives. It is never recorded or saved, on either phone, and never passes through a server.

What people nearby can see

When you host a conversation, your phone broadcasts the display name you chose so the other person can find you. Like any Bluetooth broadcast, anyone nearby scanning for Bluetooth devices can see that name while you are hosting. Nobody can join without your say-so: you accept each person, or they enter the PIN you set.

The voice link between the two phones is not encrypted. Someone close by with specialized Bluetooth equipment could, in principle, receive the audio while you talk. Use Whisper for conversations you would be comfortable having out loud nearby.

What stays on your phone

So you can rejoin with one tap, Whisper remembers the name of the person you last talked to, and their PIN if they used one, in the app's own storage on your device. That is never sent anywhere. It is removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

Whisper contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send the developer anonymized crash reports under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Whisper does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
